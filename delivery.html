Delivery & pickup

Free pickup at our warehouse, or delivery to any state in Nigeria.

Free warehouse pickup

Bring your order code. You can pay cash or POS at pickup if you chose "Pay at the warehouse".

Delivery times

  • Lagos: usually 1–2 working days.
  • Other states: usually 2–5 working days, depending on the route.
  • We message you on WhatsApp when your bale is on the way, and your order page updates too.

Before delivery

Online payment (transfer or card) must be completed before we dispatch. Pay at the warehouse is for pickup orders only.

Delivery fee by state

One fee per order, whatever the number of bales.

Terms of sale

The basics of buying from us.

Orders

An order is confirmed once payment is received, or for pay-at-warehouse orders, when you collect and pay. Online orders not paid within 30 minutes are cancelled and the bales go back on sale.

Prices

Prices are in Naira and shown per bale. Delivery fees are shown at checkout before you pay.

Bale contents

Bales are second-hand clothing sorted by grade. Piece counts are approximate. The grade guide explains what to expect from each grade.

Delivery

Delivery times are estimates. See Delivery & pickup.

No refunds

All sales are final and no refunds are given once a bale is paid for. If we send the wrong category or grade, we exchange it. See our refund policy.

Frequently asked questions

Grades, payment, delivery and pickup, answered.

What do the grades mean?+

Cream is the first selection: near-new pieces with very few rejects. Grade A is good condition with light wear. Grade B is wearable with visible use and has the lowest cost per piece.

Can I see inside a bale before I pay?+

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp from the bale's page and we'll send an opening video or photos.

How many pieces are in a bale?+

It depends on the category and weight. Each bale's page shows an approximate piece count where we have one.

How do I pay?+

Choose Bank transfer at checkout and a secure Paystack window shows you an account number. Send the exact amount from your bank app and it confirms automatically, no receipt needed. You can also pay by card or USSD in the same window, or by cash or POS when you collect at the warehouse.

How long does delivery take?+

Lagos orders usually arrive in 1–2 days. Other states take 2–5 days depending on the route. We'll message you when your bale is on the way.

How much is delivery?+

It depends on your state. You'll see the exact fee at checkout before you pay.

Can I pay when my bale is delivered?+

Pay on site is for warehouse pickup only. For delivery, pay online or by transfer before we dispatch.

I paid by transfer. What next?+

Nothing. The payment window confirms your transfer automatically and your order page changes to "Payment received". If you closed the window before it confirmed, open your order page and it will update once the transfer lands.

Do you sell in bulk to traders?+

Yes. For large orders message us on WhatsApp and we'll talk about pricing.

Refund policy

All sales are final. Here's how to buy with confidence.

All sales are final

We do not offer refunds on any bale once it has been paid for. Please make sure it's the right bale before you pay.

Why no refunds?

Bales are sold sealed, graded and priced by weight. Once a bale leaves our warehouse we can't resell it as a sealed bale, so every sale is final.

Check before you pay

  • Read the grade, weight, origin and piece count on the bale's page.
  • Ask us on WhatsApp for an opening video of the exact bale.
  • Visit the warehouse to see stock in person.

If we send the wrong bale

If you receive a different category or grade from what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours with your order code and an opening video. We'll swap it for the bale you paid for. This is an exchange, not a refund.

Failed payments

If your bank debits you but the payment fails, that money was never received by us. Paystack or your bank reverses it automatically, usually within 24 hours.
